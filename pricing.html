Pricing FAQ

What counts as one audit?
One audit = one ad copy run through the rule engine, producing one report. Re-running the same ad counts again — but since the engine is deterministic, you'll get the identical report.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly plans cancel in one click and stay active until the end of the billing period. (Billing is handled through Stripe once checkout is connected.)
Do unused audits roll over?
No — audit quotas reset each month. Most agencies use far fewer than 50 once their ad templates are cleaned up, which is the point.
Is there a free trial of Pro Agency?
The Starter plan is free forever and runs the exact same engine — it's the trial. Upgrade when you need volume or white-label client reports.
