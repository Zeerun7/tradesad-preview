Frequently asked questions

Is this legal advice? Will my ad definitely be approved?
No — and any tool that promises that is lying to you. TradesAd is a pre-screen: it flags language that commonly triggers disapprovals under published Google, Meta, EPA, and FTC rules. The ad platforms make the final call. When in doubt, have a human media buyer review before launching.
Why rule-based instead of AI?
Because rule failures are legible: when the engine misses something, we add a pattern, add a regression test, and bump the policy version — the miss becomes a permanent fix. AI failures are mysteries you can't patch. For compliance-adjacent work, deterministic and versioned beats a confident black box.
Does my ad copy leave my browser?
No. The entire engine runs locally in the page — there are no network calls, no accounts required to run an audit, and nothing is uploaded or stored.
What happens when ad platforms change their policies?
The pattern library is versioned (currently v2026-09-29). When guidance changes, we add or adjust patterns, re-run the full regression suite, and ship a new version. Every report stamps which version judged it, so old reports stay honest.
Can I put my agency's branding on reports?
Yes — the Pro Agency plan ($197/mo) includes white-label PDF reports with your branding, ready to hand to clients.
